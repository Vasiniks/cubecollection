## 2. Blocking

Nothing is `published`, so nothing is literally blocked yet. Each of these will block, or will
silently defeat the rule that should block it, at reconciliation. Rule numbers are the rules
that fire or are evaded.

### B1 — A `confirmed` resting on one tier 2 source, five times over — **rule 9**

| File | Pointer | Sources behind `confirmed` |
|---|---|---|
| `gan-axis-coatings.yml` | `/findings/3` "Soft Texture Coated" | 1 × TheCubicle (tier 2) |
| `gan-axis-sticker-application.yml` | `/findings/0` "Stickered (Master tier)" | 2 × TheCubicle — **one publisher** |
| `gan-axis-sticker-application.yml` | `/findings/1` "Unstickered (DIY)" | 2 × TheCubicle — **one publisher** |
| `gan-axis-colourways.yml` | `/findings/3` "Dual-WR Limited Edition" | 1 × TheCubicle (tier 2) |
| `gan-axis-colourways.yml` | `/findings/5` "Christmas Edition (GAN356 ME)" | 1 × TheCubicle (tier 2) |

Rule 9 permits `confirmed` on tier 1, or on **two genuinely independent** tier 2 sources.
`vocab/source-kinds.yml` derives `retailer` → tier 2. Two listings from the same retailer are
one source (DATA_MODEL §5.2, RESEARCH_SPEC §3.2), so the two-source cases are single-source
cases. Every one of these is `probable` at best.

`/findings/3` in colourways is the most consequential: it is the only stated run size in the
entire pass (**1,111 units**), `edition.limited.run_size` is `x-critical`, and the figure rests
on one retailer's marketing paragraph rather than on GAN.

**What a human should do.** Downgrade each to `probable` or find genuine tier 1. For Dual-WR,
require GAN's own page before the run size enters a record; the same excerpt also asserts two
dated WCA world records, which are verifiable against WCA results and should not be taken from
retailer copy. Do not let a second TheCubicle URL be added to satisfy rule 9 — the script cannot
tell same-publisher listings apart, so this rule is only as good as the human reading it.

### B2 — Corroboration slots filled with a source that does not carry the claim — **rules 7, 9**

`gan-axis-sticker-application.yml` `/findings/0/evidence/1` cites
`thecubicle.com/products/gans-core-v4` — a **spare part** listing — and its "excerpt" is not a
quotation at all but the researcher's inference: *"Confirms the same-era product naming pattern
… establishing the Gans 356 Air ran multiple named sub-editions (Master, Advanced, Standard)."*
A replacement core page cannot establish an edition-tier structure. The tier structure is
already carried by `/evidence/0`, which quotes the Master page verbatim. This second item adds
nothing and exists only to make the finding look corroborated — which is what lifts it to
`confirmed`.

**What a human should do.** Strike the evidence item. The finding stands at `probable` on
`/evidence/0` alone.

### B3 — Site navigation chrome cited as page content, supporting four `confirmed` claims — **rules 7, 9**

`https://www.gancube.cn/en/patents/` is cited in three files. I fetched it. The Limited Edition
name list **is** present and the excerpt **is** verbatim — the researcher transcribed it
accurately. But it is the site's **global navigation menu**, rendered identically on every page
of gancube.cn; the page's own content is GAN's patent/technology marketing (GMS, GES+, IPG,
GTN). Two separate problems follow:

1. **The citation misdirects.** Anyone checking the claim lands on a patents page. The URL that
   actually renders the category should be cited, or the nav documented as site-wide chrome.
2. **It is being asked to establish more than it can.** A nav menu is tier 1 evidence that
   *GAN's own site uses these names as catalog entries under a "Limited Edition" heading*. It is
   evidence of nothing else — not model pairing, not dates, not configuration.

Where it overreaches:

- `gan-axis-colourways.yml` `/findings/0/likely_model` — `confidence: confirmed` for *"spans
  GAN11, GAN12, GAN13, GAN14, GAN15, GAN330 across at least 2020-2026"*. The file's **own**
  `/findings/0/open_questions/1` says the opposite: *"proves the NAMES exist as a GAN-declared
  category but not which specific model each pairs with, nor release dates."* A disagreement
  stated in prose and resolved silently in the field beside it.
- `gan-axis-coatings.yml` `/findings/0/evidence/1` — uses the nav to corroborate *"the
  UV-coated/spring/black-internals configuration is catalogued by GAN as its own line"*. The nav
  shows `GAN15 黑核` and `GAN15 Maglev` as two entries. That supports "GAN15 黑核 is a distinct
  catalog entry". It does not support the UV/spring/black-internals bundling.
- `gan-axis-magnet-configurations.yml` `/findings/0/evidence/0` cites the **same URL** for the
  GMS technology text — and that one is correct, because the GMS passage is genuinely on that
  page. The contrast is the point: the same source is used well once and overreached three times.

Nothing citing this URL is preserved. GAN's nav changes with every product drop.

**What a human should do.** Keep the naming claim, at tier 1, cited to the page that renders the
category and captured (`archive_url` or `local_capture`) before it moves. Strip the model-span,
date, and configuration inferences back to `unknown` pending per-product pages.

### B4 — Aftermarket listings used as evidence for factory configurations — **§4.3, rule 17**

`gan-axis-coatings.yml` scopes itself to *"factory surface-coating axis … Aftermarket coatings …
are staged separately"*. Both GAN 11 M Pro entries then cite **Cubicle Custom** pages:

- `/findings/3` "Soft Texture Coated" ← `thecubicle.com/products/cubicle-custom-gan-11-m-pro-3x3-soft-texture-coated`
- the UV entry's model list ← the page already in `data/sources/thecubicle-cubicle-custom-gan11-m-pro-uv.yml`,
  whose own excerpt reads *"our customizable version of GAN's 2020 flagship 3x3"*

`gan-axis-magnet-configurations.yml` `/findings/1/evidence/1` cites the same Soft Texture
Cubicle Custom page for GAN's core design.

Under §4.3 these products are variants with `config.coating: aftermarket` and
`coating_applied_by`, joined by `modified_from`, and rule 17 requires that relationship to carry
a tier 1–2 attestation. Reading them as factory finishes puts a service's treatment onto the base
variant — the §4.2/§4.3 failure the boundary rule exists to prevent.

In fairness: the Soft Texture excerpt does say *"We include the original GAN Soft Texture Coated
center cap in the packaging as well"*, which is real evidence that **GAN** made such a cap. That
is a legitimate inference and "Soft Texture Coated" may well be a genuine fourth GAN coating. It
is not `confirmed`, and it cannot be established from a service's product page.

Compounding it: the UV and Soft Texture excerpts are near-identical TheCubicle boilerplate with
the coating name substituted —

> "…unable to remove the GAN logo due to the **UV Coated** surface. Instead, we provide a standard
> plastic GAN 11 M Pro center cap with a Cubicle Pro Shop logo." *(in `data/sources/`)*
> "…unable to remove the GAN logo due to the **Soft Texture Coated** surface. Instead, we provide a
> standard plastic GAN 11 M Pro center cap with a Cubicle Pro Shop logo." *(staged)*

Per §5.2 that is one template, not two observations.

**What a human should do.** Re-file both GAN 11 M Pro coating entries as aftermarket, and require
a GAN-hosted listing before "Soft Texture Coated" is recorded as a factory finish. The staging
file's own `note` correctly refers the vocab question (`other` vs a new value) to the schema
owner rather than deciding it — that judgement was right and should be preserved.

### B5 — Every staged source is excerpt-only, on a premise I could not reproduce — **rule 8**

No evidence item in any of the four staging files carries `archive_url` or `local_capture`.
`gan-axis-coatings.yml` justifies this at `/scope`:

> "web.archive.org (the CDX API and snapshot host) was unreachable for this entire research
> session (connection refused / timeout on every attempt, including a final retry at session end)"

In this same session, on this machine: `npm run wayback -- list` returned results repeatedly; the
CDX API answered 13 queries; I fetched and text-verified two full snapshots. The snapshot host
*did* start refusing me after roughly fifteen fetches, with an explicit page reading *"flagged it
as suspected abusive bot traffic."* That is **rate limiting under six concurrent agents**, not
unreachability — and `gan-axis-colourways.yml` `/findings/6/evidence/0` confirms it, because it
reports `npm run wayback -- prefix` **working** (that is the CDX API) in the same session the
header calls CDX unreachable.

This matters because `config.coating` is `x-critical` and, per rule 6's inheritance clause, is
the one critical field with no model counterpart — it must be attested on **every** variant. Rule 8
then requires each backing source to preserve. As staged, not one coating attestation can be made.

**What a human should do.** Re-run the archive pass with paced requests before accepting
excerpt-only preservation across four axes. Treat the "unreachable" note as unverified and correct
it. Rule 34's preservation mix will otherwise record an avoidable slide as if it were a constraint.

### M5 — Boundary: a coating-only axis will collapse crossed configurations

**What a human should do.** Enumerate the Air family as one unit before any of it is minted, and

## 5. What this wave got right

Recording this because the findings above are dense and the baseline is genuinely good.

- **Every cited archive capture is real.** Twelve of twelve verified against CDX at the exact
  timestamp. Two verified verbatim against fetched page text. Nothing fabricated.
- **The superlative guard held at source level.** *"first mass-produced magnetic flagship cube"*
  (GAN), *"world's first mass produced magnetic 3x3"* (TheCubicle) are each flagged tier 4 for the
  comparative claim in their own `reliability_note`, per §5.2 rule 2. Correct, and it must survive
  into `significance` prose, which is where it will be tested.
- **`monster-go-official-home.yml`** establishes the parent relationship from an ICP filing number
  matching gancube.cn's rather than from naming resemblance. That is documentary evidence, and the
  distinction is drawn explicitly in `/reliability_note`.
- **`gan-axis-magnet-configurations.yml` `/findings/2/open_questions/0`** is the best judgement in
  the tree: it notices the source says magnet *slots were removed* (a moulding difference), applies
  the §4.2 test, sees that it contradicts §4.1's variant-level default for magnet configuration,
